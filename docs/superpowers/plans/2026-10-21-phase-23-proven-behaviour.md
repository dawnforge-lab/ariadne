## Review Focus

1. **A robots.txt that disallows a huge URL space** (`Disallow: /search`, faceted `?filter=` links on every page) — blocked rows must be capped by their own budget, never consume the `maxPages` fetch budget, and the overflow must be counted on the crawl row, not dropped. (Task 3 test.)
2. **Documents with no explicit `<head>`/`<body>`**, and heads containing `<noscript>`, `<template>`, `<script>`, `<style>`, whitespace, comments — the implicit-close machine must not report a correctly placed canonical as outside the head. (Task 5 tests, cheerio/parse5 parity on each.)
3. **Pages that were not rendered, or whose render failed** — divergence rules must be silent and the render-coverage evidence must keep saying what was sampled; "not rendered" must never read as "no divergence". (Task 6 test.)
4. **Pagination without `rel=prev`** (`?page=2` linked only by anchors) and page 1 carrying only `rel=next` — neither is excluded from duplicate rules; only pages declaring `rel=prev` are. (Task 7 test.)
5. **Pre-0020 rows** (all new columns null) re-judged under 0.18.0 — no new rule fires on a null, and the kept lavishalice crawls re-judge without error. (Gate task, plus one null-row test per new rule.)
